Hi 👋, I am Rajesh. I am a computational scientist with a variety of interests. My core training and expertise is in fluid dynamics, numerical methods and high-performance scientific computing. I have transitioned to lead software development in various domains such as Cybersecurity, Bioinformatics (https://grepbio.com/) and enterprise general-purpose software (https://appgrape.com/).  My approach to developing robust, extensible software is through strict type-safety, highly fluid abstrations and defensive programming. 

I closely follow Semantic technologies, Knowledge Graphs, AI/ML Deep learning and frequently incorporate them in my work.
